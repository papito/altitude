@(paths: String, crushed: String, blown: String, shows: String = "", id: String = "", disabled: String = "") @* The Histogram (CONTEXT.md), the one markup of it: Curation's strip under each stage pane (htmx/curation_set), the Edit panel's (includes/edit_panel) and the style guide's specimen, styled by includes/histogram.css. It is a button, the Clipping blink's switch, so the Alpine component it is rendered under gives `clippingBlink` and `toggleClippingBlink()`; as it holds no text of its own, its `aria-label` names it and its title says what it does. The parameters are Alpine expressions of that component: `paths` the Histogram's paths by channel, an object of `luminance`, `red`, `green` and `blue` (js/common/histogram.js: `channelPaths`, or `NO_PATHS` for none), drawn in the `viewBox` that module draws in (`HISTOGRAM_BOX`), the three colors as filled shapes that blend where they overlap and the luminance as a line over them; `crushed` and `blown` the labels of its top corners, the fractions of the pixels crushed (left) and blown (right), an empty one showing nothing; and, where there is one, `shows` whether it is shown and `disabled` whether there is nothing to press for. `id` is the button's ID, for a script that finds it. *@
